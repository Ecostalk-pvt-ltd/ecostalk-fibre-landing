Our story

Value from what the harvest leaves behind.

Ecostalk Fibre Pvt. Ltd. is a banana fibre manufacturer based in Bhusawal, Jalgaon, Maharashtra. Our business is built on one idea: banana plant residue is a resource.

Once bananas are harvested, the plant's pseudostem is typically cut down and left as agricultural waste. The pseudostem contains natural fibre. By extracting and processing that fibre, we aim to create a useful material from crop residue while supporting a more circular economy.

As a natural fibre supplier, our goal is to serve customers, distributors, exporters and business partners who are looking for sustainable material options.

Our mission

Our Mission

At Ecostalk Fibre Pvt. Ltd., our mission is to harness the potential of banana plants by converting agricultural waste into high-quality, sustainable banana fibre.

We are committed to:

  • SustainabilityPromoting eco-friendly alternatives to conventional materials.
  • InnovationDeveloping advanced and efficient banana fibre extraction and processing solutions.
  • Farmer EmpowermentCreating additional value from banana crop residues and supporting agricultural communities.
  • Quality ExcellenceDelivering reliable, high-quality banana fibre to domestic and international markets.
  • Global ImpactContributing to a circular economy by reducing agricultural waste and promoting renewable resources.

Our vision

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized leader in sustainable banana fibre manufacturing by transforming agricultural waste into valuable, eco-friendly products and creating a greener, more sustainable future for generations to come.
